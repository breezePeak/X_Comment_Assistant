    • Responsibility
      October 27, 2026

      The LEGO Group teams up with Sesame Street to inspire young children to fall in love with nature

      The LEGO Group and Sesame Workshop, the global nonprofit behind Sesame Street, have launched three new videos, funded by the LEGO Foundation, encouraging children aged 4 to 6 to discover the natural world through play.
      Featuring Elmo, Cookie Monster and other Sesame Street friends, the videos introduce children to bats, butterflies and worms before inviting them to build habitats using LEGO bricks and everyday craft materials. The LEGO Group’s free activity guides and printables help families and educators extend the experience through hands-on play at home and in community settings.

      Builders After All: The LEGO Group and DAFT PUNK™ announce partnership – blending worlds of music, culture and creative experimentation

      The LEGO Group and DAFT PUNK™ are proud to announce a new partnership that aims to inspire and remix fandoms, with the Daft Punk robots entering the LEGO universe. Spanning product, content and brand experiences, the collaboration celebrates the creativity and communities that have shaped the legacy of both brands.

      October 15, 2026

      Ciao, Venice: The LEGO Group floats new Icons set

      The LEGO Group reveals the LEGO® Icons Venetian Neighbourhood (11388) set. With 5,682 pieces, this is one of the most intricate city dioramas in the LEGO Icons collection and marks the first time the LEGO Group has created a cityscape-style display model of this scale, inviting builders to capture the romance, rhythm and remarkable architecture of Venice with LEGO elements – no passport required.

      September 28, 2026

      The LEGO Group and Toei Animation unite for the first time to summon the epic LEGO® ICONS Dragon Ball Shenron & Goku set

      The LEGO Group announces a brand-new partnership with Toei Animation, the legendary anime studio behind the iconic Dragon Ball franchise created by Akira Toriyama. Through this collaboration, the LEGO Group will bring the action and adventure of the Dragon Ball universe to the LEGO® portfolio for the very first time this year, with more to come in 2027 and beyond.

    • Press Releases
      September 24, 2026

      The LEGO Group announces next phase of growth for Monterrey, Mexico manufacturing site

      The LEGO Group today announced plans to further strengthen its manufacturing footprint in the Americas through a more than US $400 million investment in its Monterrey, Mexico factory. The expansion will add new packing and warehousing capabilities to the site, supporting long-term growth in the region.
